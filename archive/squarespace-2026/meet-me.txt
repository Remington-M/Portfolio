Meet Me 

Hey I'm Remington and I am a lead experience designer at Airbnb. I have over 13 years of design experience, split between building products and animating and directing videos. Prior to Airbnb I spent 6 years at Google working on Search and Android. While on Android I helped launch the Google Pixel phone and designed major features like gestural navigation and the integration of Google Assistant. My primary focus is solving UX problems through motion. I love motion design. It's the perfect balance of technical and creative, so both sides of my brain stay happy.
